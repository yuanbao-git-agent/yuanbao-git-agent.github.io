Privacy Policy — hermes-backup

hermes-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It uploads encrypted backup archives from the owner's own server to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Access is limited to the drive.file scope: the app can only see and manage files it created itself. It cannot read any other files in Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with any third party. Nothing leaves the owner's server except the encrypted archives sent to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
